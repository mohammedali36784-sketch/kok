About Velox

Brand Brief

  • Brand Name: Velox
  • Industry: Sportswear & Athletic Apparel
  • Meaning: Fast, efficient, and powerful
  • Mission: High-quality sportswear for peak athletic performance
  • Vision: Leading sports brand in the Middle East — football, basketball, tennis & gym

Target Audience

  • Age: 18–35 years
  • Interests: Sports, fitness, and outdoor activities
  • Income: Middle to high income
  • Geography: Urban and suburban areas

Marketing Strategy

  • Social media on Instagram, Facebook & Twitter
  • Influencer partnerships with fitness athletes
  • Content marketing — workout tips & guides
  • Personalized email campaigns
  • Sponsorship of local sports events
  • Collaborations with gyms and fitness centers
  • SEO and online advertising
  • Pop-up stores and experiential marketing

Brand Positioning

  • High performance meets modern design
  • Sits between global luxury brands and low-quality local options
  • Balance of quality, price, and identity
